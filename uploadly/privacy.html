Uploadly Privacy Policy

Effective date: [YYYY-MM-DD] · Applies to the Uploadly Android app (com.appsauto.uploadly)

In short: your photos and documents are processed on your phone and are never uploaded by Uploadly. The only data that leaves your device is what the Google AdMob advertising service collects to show ads, under the consent choices you make.

Who we are

Uploadly is published by [DEVELOPER NAME] ("we"). Contact: [SUPPORT EMAIL].

Your photos and files

  • Compressing, resizing, converting, passport-photo cropping and image-to-PDF all run entirely on your device. Uploadly has no server and does not upload, store or view your files.
  • You pick images through the Android photo picker; Uploadly only sees the images you select. It does not request access to your whole gallery or camera.
  • Results are kept in the app's private storage so they appear in History. You can delete them at any time in Settings → Clear all history, or by uninstalling the app. Copies you saved to a folder or shared are yours and are not affected.
  • Location data (GPS) embedded in a photo is removed from the files Uploadly creates.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Uploadly is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect:

DataPurpose
Advertising ID and device identifiersShowing ads, frequency capping, measurement, fraud prevention
Approximate location (from IP address)Ad targeting and measurement
Ad interactions and diagnosticsAd delivery and performance

This data goes to Google, not to us, and is handled under Google's policy on how it uses data from partner apps and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices: in the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later in Settings → Ad privacy choices. On any Android device you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Usage statistics

This version of Uploadly does not send usage statistics or crash reports. The app contains a switch, Settings → Share anonymous usage statistics, prepared for a future version. If we enable it, it will only report which tools are used and whether they worked (never photos, file names or personal data), this policy will be updated first, and you will be able to turn it off.

What we do not do

  • No account, sign-in, contacts, camera, microphone or precise location.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • All network traffic from the ads SDK is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Children

Uploadly is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that sent data to an ad provider, contact us and we will help.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data on our side, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data held by Google for advertising, use the controls above or Google My Ad Center. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Moroccan Law 09-08) you may also contact your local data-protection authority.

Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date. Significant changes will also be announced in the app.
